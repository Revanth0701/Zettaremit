# Supported Currencies and Transfer Corridors

## Supported Currency Corridor
- **Source Currency**: Indian Rupee (INR).
- **Target / Recipient Currency**: United States Dollar (USD).
- **Single Supported Corridor**: ZettaRemit exclusively supports sending **Indian Rupees (INR) to United States Dollars (USD)**.
- **Strict Directionality**: Transfers are strictly unidirectional from INR to USD. Sending USD to INR is not supported.

## Explicitly Unsupported Transfer Directions and Currency Pairs
- **US Dollars to Euros (USD to EUR)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **Euros to US Dollars (EUR to USD)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **Euros to British Pounds (EUR to GBP)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **British Pounds to Euros (GBP to EUR)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **US Dollars to Indian Rupees (USD to INR)**: Strictly **NOT** supported (no inbound remittances to India).
- **British Pounds to Indian Rupees (GBP to INR)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **Indian Rupees to British Pounds (INR to GBP)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **Indian Rupees to Euros (INR to EUR)**: Strictly **NOT** supported.
- **Other Global Currencies**: Transfers involving Canadian Dollars (CAD), Australian Dollars (AUD), Japanese Yen (JPY), Singapore Dollars (SGD), or United Arab Emirates Dirhams (AED) are completely unsupported.
- **Cryptocurrencies**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC, or any other digital or crypto assets are strictly unsupported.

## Rules for Answering Currency Corridor Questions
- **Direct Answer**: If a user asks whether ZettaRemit supports any currency pair or direction other than INR to USD, the answer is **"No"**.
- **Preserve Currency Direction**: Maintain the exact source and target currencies requested by the user. If asked about "US Dollars to Euros" (USD to EUR), state that ZettaRemit does not support US Dollars to Euros (USD to EUR). Do not flip the direction to EUR to USD.
- **Clarify Sole Corridor**: Always clarify that ZettaRemit exclusively simulates outbound transfers from Indian Rupees (INR) to United States Dollars (USD).
